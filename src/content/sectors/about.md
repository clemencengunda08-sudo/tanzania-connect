# About Tanzania

Tanzania is the largest country in East Africa, formed in 1964 by the union of Tanganyika and the Zanzibar archipelago. With >67 million people, >125 ethnic groups, and a territory covering 945,087 km² (including 60,000 km² of inland water), it is home to the Great Wildebeest Migration, Mount Kilimanjaro (the highest point on the African continent at 5,895 m), the Serengeti, the Ngorongoro Crater, Zanzibar, and some of the world's oldest human-ancestor fossils. Politically stable since independence, it has a single dominant political party (CCM) but peaceful transitions of power within CCM.

## Key Facts

- **Official name**: United Republic of Tanzania (Jamhuri ya Muungano wa Tanzania).
- **Independence**: Tanganyika 1961 (from UK), Zanzibar 1963, Union 1964.
- **Capital**: Dodoma (legislative and political), Dar es Salaam (commercial and diplomatic hub, many embassies still there).
- **Head of State**: President (currently Samia Suluhu Hassan, sworn in 19 March 2021 — first female president in the country).
- **Languages**: Swahili (national), English (official in higher courts, universities, business).
- **Currency**: Tanzanian shilling (TZS / TSh). Coins (50, 100, 200, 500 TSh), notes (500, 1,000, 2,000, 5,000, 10,000 TSh).
- **Time zone**: EAT (UTC+3) all year — no daylight saving.
- **Driving**: left-hand side, like UK.
- **Country code**: +255. International airport codes: DAR (JNIA), JRO (Kilimanjaro), ZNZ (Zanzibar), MBI (Songwe), MWZ (Mwanza).
- **Religion**: Christian ~63%, Muslim ~34% (Zanzibar 95%+ Muslim), traditional/other ~3%.
- **Major ethnic groups**: Sukuma, Chagga, Haya, Nyamwezi, Hehe, Gogo, Maasai, Makonde, Yao, Bena, Zigua.
- **Largest religions** in the political sphere: interfaith peace councils operate.
- **Tanzania People's Defence Force (TPDF)**: military; SADC/EAC member; significant UN peacekeeping contributor.
- **National parks**: 22 under TANAPA, 4 game reserves under TAWA, 1 conservation area (Ngorongoro) under NCAA.

## Statistics

- **Population**: ~67 million (2024 est.).
- **Area**: 945,087 km² (land 885,800 km², water 59,287 km² — lakes Victoria, Tanganyika, Nyasa, etc.).
- **Highest point**: Mount Kilimanjaro, Uhuru Peak, 5,895 m.
- **Lowest point**: Indian Ocean, 0 m.
- **Lake Tanganyika**: second-deepest lake in the world (~1,470 m), contains 18% of Earth's fresh surface water.
- **GDP (2024 est.)**: ~USD 75 billion; per capita ~USD 1,100.
- **Life expectancy**: ~66 years.
- **Adult literacy**: ~78%.
- **Mobile penetration**: ~85% of adults.
- **Internet users**: ~30+ million (~50% of population).
- **Tourism**: ~1.7 million annual visitors; ~USD 3 billion+ in receipts.

## Headline Geography

- **Northern circuit**: Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Tarangire, Lake Manyara, Kilimanjaro, Arusha.
- **Southern circuit**: Nyerere (Selous), Ruaha, Mikumi, Udzungwa Mountains.
- **Western Tanzania**: Gombe Stream, Mahale Mountains, Katavi, Lake Tanganyika.
- **Coast & islands**: Dar es Salaam, Bagamoyo, Pangani, Tanga, Mafia Island, Zanzibar (Unguja), Pemba.
- **National parks**: 22, including Tarangire, Lake Manyara, Ngorongoro Conservation Area, Serengeti, Kilimanjaro, Ruaha, Nyerere, Mikumi, Gombe Stream, Mahale, Katavi, Kitulo, Saadani, Mkomazi, Udzungwa Mountains, Arusha, Tarangire, Saanane, Burigi-Chato, Ibanda-Kyerwa, Rumanyika-Kasamwa, Uwanda.

## Government

- **Type**: unitary presidential republic.
- **Executive**: President (head of state and government), Vice President, Cabinet.
- **Legislative**: Bunge (National Assembly) with 393+ members + President-appointed special seats.
- **Judicial**: Court of Appeal, High Court, district and primary courts. Also the new specialised Commercial, Land, and Labour courts.
- **Tanzania and Zanzibar**: Zanzibar is semi-autonomous with its own President, House of Representatives, and Revolutionary Council. Union matters (defence, foreign affairs, currency) are federal.

## At-a-glance Tourist Banner

- **Climb Kilimanjaro** — Machame, Marangu, Lemosho routes. 5–9 days.
- **Safari in the Serengeti** — calving season (Jan–Mar), river crossings (Jul–Sep).
- **Ngorongoro Crater descent** — half-day tour, ~5,000 large mammals visible.
- **Zanzibar beach + Stone Town** — Stone Town UNESCO, Forodhani Gardens, Kendwa, Nungwi.
- **Tarangire + Lake Manyara** — day trips from Arusha.
- **Mahale or Gombe** — chimpanzee trekking, Lake Tanganyika.
- **Olduvai Gorge & Laetoli** — Leakey hominid sites, Ngorongoro Conservation Area.
- **Chome Forest (Usambaras)** — chimpanzees, chameleons, hiking.
