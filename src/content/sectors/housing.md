# Housing & Real Estate

Dar es Salaam is East Africa's fastest-growing city in real-estate terms, with new serviced apartments, gated compounds, and tower developments rising across the prime peninsula. Dodoma (the political capital) and Arusha (the safari hub) are secondary markets with strong rental demand from government staff, NGOs, and tourism operators. Land is held on leasehold and is the single biggest factor in pricing — most transactions are for sub-leases on land originally allocated in the 1970s–90s.

## Key Facts

- **Land tenure**: All land in Tanzania is public. Private rights exist only as leaseholds (33, 66, or 99 years) issued by the President or local authorities. Foreigners can only own land through TISEZA-issued derivative rights (investment land, residential for pensioners, etc.).
- **Prime residential areas (Dar es Salaam)**:
  - **Masaki / Oyster Bay / Msasani** — international school zone, embassies, top rents. Three-bedroom house USD 2,500–5,000/month.
  - **Mikocheni / Ada Estate** — middle-upper, popular with NGO and embassy staff. USD 1,500–3,000/month.
  - **Kariakoo / Upanga / Ilala** — central, mixed-use, lower price. USD 600–1,500/month for 2–3 bed.
  - **Kigamboni (south of Kigamboni bridge)** — newer apartments, growing fast. USD 800–2,000/month.
  - **Mbezi Beach / Kunduchi (north coast)** — beachfront villas, residential estates. USD 1,500–4,000/month.
  - **Bagamoyo Road corridor** — apartment boom, more affordable new builds. USD 700–1,800/month.
- **Arusha**: Njiro, Sakina, Themi — USD 600–1,800/month.
- **Dodoma**: new government town, high rental demand. USD 500–1,500/month.
- **Purchase price** (leasehold): 1,000–5,000 USD per m² in prime Dar; apartments USD 100,000–500,000; villas USD 250,000–1.5M+.
- **Annual rent yields**: gross 6–9% in Dar; higher in tourist areas (Arusha/Zanzibar).
- **Major developers**: Kibo Real Estate, AMS Properties, HYT (Hassanali Yusufali & Co), Quality Group, Mikocheni Plaza, Mbezi Beach residents' developments.
- **Listing portals**: Jiji.co.tz, ZoomTanzania, PropertyPro, HamariBora, HassConsult cross-border.

## Statistics

- Urbanisation rate: ~35% nationally; Dar es Salaam metro: ~8 million (and growing 4–5%/year).
- Housing shortage estimated at 3+ million units nationwide; demand concentrated in Dar, Dodoma, Mwanza, Arusha.
- Average mortgage-to-GDP ratio: <1% (one of the lowest in East Africa). Most housing is bought cash.
- Service charges in prime compounds: USD 1–2/m²/month.
