# Entertainment, Guides & About

Beyond wildlife and beaches, Tanzania offers a rich cultural, music, and lifestyle scene. From Sauti za Busara (the East African music festival held in Zanzibar) to the National Museum of Tanzania in Dar, from nightclubs featuring bongo flava stars to art galleries in Stone Town, the country's entertainment and cultural scene rewards curious visitors and residents alike. This page is a hub for the rest of the Tanzania Connect guide.

## Music & Nightlife

- **Bongo flava** — Tanzania's homegrown pop genre blending hip-hop, R&B, dancehall, taarab and traditional ngoma. Major names: **Diamond Platnumz** (WCB Wasafi, mega-stars of East Africa), Ali Kiba, Harmonize, Rayvanny, Nandy, Mbosso, Zuchu, Lava Lava.
- **Taarab** — Swahili coastal music, Arab/Indian/Bantu fusion. Major performer: East African Melody.
- **Singeli** — fast electronic dance music from Dar's streets, ~180 BPM, lyrics in Swahili slang. Stars: Mabere, S2Kizzy, Mwana FA.
- **Ngoma** — traditional drums, still played at weddings, mhiki initiations, and cultural events.
- **Dar es Salaam clubs**: Riviera, Coco Beach, Bwawani, New Africa, Q-BAR, Limelight, Level 8, Samsara (kite, Slipway).
- **Zanzibar**: Forodhani Gardens night food market (evening), Dhow Bar, Mtoni Marine, Zanzibar Coffee House, Mercury nightclub, House of Spices.
- **Live music venues**: Alliance Française (Dar, classical/jazz), National Archives amphitheatre.
- **Comedy & spoken word**: Kigamboni, Kijiwe cha 23, and university open-mic nights.

## Arts, Museums, Galleries

- **National Museum of Tanzania (NMT)** — Dar es Salaam (Kariakoo). Includes the Leakey-era Olduvai Gorge fossils, ethnographic galleries, and the 1961 Uhuru Torch.
- **Village Museum** — Bagamoyo Road, Dar — open-air museum with replica bomas of 16 Tanzanian ethnic groups.
- **Nyumba ya Sanaa (House of Art)** — Dar — community-based African art and craft centre.
- **Mikocheni** — the old print/photography gallery scene.
- **Stone Town museums, Zanzibar**: **Beit al-Ajaib (House of Wonders)**, Palace Museum (Beit el Sahel), Old Fort, Hamamni Persian Baths, Peace Memorial Museum (Cathedral of Christ Church), Dr. David Livingstone's house, Freddie Mercury statue and original family home.
- **Arusha**: Cultural Heritage Art Gallery, Central Market.
- **Bagamoyo**: Catholic Mission Museum, Kaole Ruins (13th-century), Livingstone memorial tower.
- **Kilwa Kisiwani & Songo Mnara** (UNESCO) — medieval Swahili trading-post ruins, reachable by boat from Kilwa Masoko.

## Festivals & Events

- **Sauti za Busara** (Feb, Zanzibar) — pan-African music festival, 4 days, 400+ artists. busaramusic.com.
- **ZIFF — Zanzibar International Film Festival** (Jul) — Dhow Countries Film Festival. ziff.or.tz.
- **Mwaka Kogwa** (Jul, Zanzibar) — Persian/Zanzibari New Year fire festival in Makunduchi.
- **Nane Nane** (Aug 8) — Farmers' Day, Nane Nane grounds, Dodoma.
- **Saba Saba** (Jul 7) — Dar es Salaam International Trade Fair.
- **Uhuru Day** (Dec 9) — Independence Day parade and celebrations.
- **Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Hajj** — public holidays, especially in Zanzibar.
- **Christmas, Good Friday, Easter Monday** — major public holidays in mainland.

## Guides — Suggested Reading

- **Bradt Tanzania** (Philip Briggs) — the gold-standard guidebook.
- **Lonely Planet Tanzania** — solid general coverage.
- **Rough Guide to Tanzania** — good cultural depth.
- **"The Tree Where Man Was Born"** (Peter Matthiessen) — classic East Africa nature writing.
- **"Born Free"** (Joy Adamson) — George and Joy Adamson, Tanzania's wildlife legacy.
- **"Going Solo" (Roald Dahl)**, **"The Old Man and the Sea"** (Hemingway) — classic African setting.
- **Magazines**: *The Citizen* (English daily), *Daily News* (govt-owned, English), *Mwanaspoti* (Swahili sports), *Sunday News*.
- **Newsletters**: *The EastAfrican*, *The Guardian Tanzania* (IPP Media), *The Chanzo* (digital), *Tanzania Invest* (business).
