# Infrastructure & Energy

Tanzania has spent the past decade on the largest infrastructure build-out in its history: the 2,115 MW Julius Nyerere Hydropower Project (JNHPP) on the Rufiji River, the 1,200+ km Standard Gauge Railway (SGR) connecting Dar es Salaam to Dodoma, Mwanza and Kigoma, and parallel upgrades to ports (Dar es Salaam, Tanga, Mtwara), airports, roads (TANROADS, TARURA), and rural electrification. The country aims to become a middle-income regional logistics and energy hub by 2030.

## Key Facts

### Energy

- **TANESCO (Tanzania Electric Supply Company Limited)** — vertically integrated national utility. Owns/operates most generation, transmission, and distribution. Reform in progress: 2024 Energy Act unbundles TPDC and opens power generation to Independent Power Producers (IPPs).
- **TPDC (Tanzania Petroleum Development Corporation)** — state upstream oil & gas company. Operates Songo Songo gas field and Kinyerezi power plants.
- **PURA (Petroleum Upstream Regulatory Authority)** — licenses and supervises upstream oil/gas operations.
- **EWURA (Energy and Water Utilities Regulatory Authority)** — downstream regulator for electricity, water, gas.
- **Julius Nyerere Hydropower Project (JNHPP)**, Stiegler's Gorge, Rufiji River — 2,115 MW, the largest hydropower plant in East Africa. Commissioned 2024–25 by Egyptian contractor Elsewedy / Arab Contractors. Estimated annual generation: ~6,300 GWh.
- **Singida Wind Power Project** — 100 MW (Phase 1, commissioned 2022) + planned expansion. Joint venture Windlab/Eni/Ubongo.
- **Huge gas reserves** in offshore Rovuma Basin (Block 2: 57+ trillion cu ft, operator Equinor/ExxonMobil; Block 1: 13+ tcf, operator Shell). Gas-to-power projects: Kinyerezi I, II, III, IV (gas-fired thermal).
- National electrification: ~75% (2024 est.); rural electrification ~55%.

### Rail

- **Tanzania Railways Corporation (TRC)** — runs legacy meter-gauge network.
- **Tanzania SGR (Standard Gauge Railway)** — electrified 1,435 mm line, 1,219 km Phase 1 (Dar es Salaam – Morogoro – Dodoma – Tabora – Kigoma). Phase 2: Tabora – Isaka – Mwanza (≈341 km). Phase 3: Tanga – Musoma. Construction by Turkish (Yapı Merkezi) and Chinese contractors; project value ~USD 14 billion+ across phases. First phase operational 2024.
- SGR electric locomotives: 10 HXD1C + 4 CKD8F imported from CRRC Zhuzhou.

### Roads

- **TANROADS** — national trunk road agency (≈33,000 km of national road network).
- **TARURA** — Tanzania Rural and Urban Roads Agency, manages district and feeder roads (~80,000 km).
- Major projects: Dodoma outer ring, Arusha–Holili (Tanzania–Kenya corridor), Bagamoyo Expressway (planned), Tanzanite Bridge (Kigamboni).

### Ports

- **Tanzania Ports Authority (TPA)** — operates Dar es Salaam (East Africa's busiest port, ~95% of Tanzania's seaborne trade), Tanga, Mtwara, and Lake Victoria ports (Mwanza, Bukoba, Kemondo Bay).
- **Dar es Salaam Maritime Gateway Project** — berth deepening, new container terminals (Berth 7,8), JV with Hutchison Ports.

### Airports

- **TAWA Airports Authority / MoTCA** manages 4 international airports: JNIA (Julius Nyerere, Dar), KIA (Kilimanjaro International, JRO), Songwe (MBI), Abeid Amani Karume (ZNZ).
- **Air Tanzania (ATCL)** — the national carrier, fleet of 9 aircraft (Boeing 787-8, 737 MAX, Q400, Dash 8, Cessna).

## Statistics

- Installed electricity capacity: ~3,200+ MW (up from 1,600 MW a decade ago), with ~2,000 MW+ in hydropower once JNHPP is fully online.
- National grid transmission lines: ~10,000+ km of 220/400 kV.
- SGR target capacity: 17 million tonnes/year freight, 1.4 million passengers/year.
- Dar es Salaam port: ~28 million tonnes throughput (2023); ~1 million TEUs.
- National road network: 33,000+ km trunk + 80,000 km district + 7,000 km regional.
