# Healthcare

Tanzania's healthcare system is a decentralised public network running from national referral hospitals down to village-level dispensaries, supplemented by faith-based organizations (FBOs) and a growing private sector. Per-capita spending is well below regional averages, but major hospital upgrades, the roll-out of universal health insurance schemes, and a 2024–2025 health-sector reform are reshaping access.

## Key Facts

- **Structure**: pyramid of village health services → health centres → district hospitals → regional referral hospitals (28) → zonal/specialised hospitals → 4 national referral hospitals.
- **National referrals**:
  - **Muhimbili National Hospital (MNH)** — 1,500 beds, Dar es Salaam, teaching hospital for Muhimbili University of Health and Allied Sciences (MUHAS).
  - **Ocean Road Cancer Institute (ORCI)** — national cancer centre, Dar es Salaam.
  - **Muhimbili Orthopaedic Institute (MOI)** — trauma and orthopaedics.
  - **Jakaya Kikwete Cardiac Institute (JKCI)** — national cardiac centre.
- **Zonal referrals**:
  - **KCMC (Kilimanjaro Christian Medical Centre)** — Moshi, 630+ beds, run by ELCT (Lutheran), premier northern referral and teaching hospital.
  - **Bugando Medical Centre (BMC)** — Mwanza, 950+ beds, Catholic-run, serves Lake Victoria zone.
  - **Mbeya Zonal Referral Hospital** — southern highlands zone.
- **Private / international**: Aga Khan Hospital (Dar), IST Clinic, TMJ Hospital, International School of Tanganyika-associated clinics, IHI (Ifakara Health Institute) for research.
- **NHIF (National Health Insurance Fund)** — mandatory public scheme for civil servants and formal employees; covers ~10% of population. Premium is income-based (~6% of salary with employer match).
- **CHF / iCHF (Community Health Fund / improved CHF)** — voluntary pre-payment scheme for the informal sector, often subsidised by local councils.
- **Strategic Health Purchasing (SHA) Act 2023** — enacted to consolidate NHIF, CHF, and other schemes into a single universal scheme under the new Tanzania Health Insurance Fund (THIF), rollout 2024–25.

## Statistics

- **Life expectancy**: ~66 years (2023 est.).
- **Physician density**: ~0.16 per 1,000 population (≈1 doctor per 6,000 people).
- **Health expenditure**: ~3.8% of GDP (one of the lowest in East Africa).
- **Maternal mortality**: ~104/100,000 live births (2023).
- **Under-5 mortality**: ~33/1,000 (2023).
- NHIF active membership: ~4.4 million principal members.
- Private health insurance penetration: <2% of population (most held by corporates / NGOs / diaspora).
