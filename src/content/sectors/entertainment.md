# Entertainment & Music

Tanzania has one of the most vibrant music and entertainment scenes in East Africa. **Bongo flava** is the dominant pop genre — a Swahili-language mix of hip-hop, R&B, dancehall, afrobeats, and traditional rhythms. The country has produced some of Africa's biggest musical stars, and a deep calendar of festivals and live-music events makes it a destination in its own right.

## Key Facts

- **Bongo flava** — Tanzania's homegrown pop. Lyrics mostly in Swahili; rhythm influenced by American hip-hop and R&B but with East African grooves.
- **Singeli** — high-BPM electronic dance music originating in Dar's streets, ~180 BPM, very percussive, Swahili slang.
- **Taarab** — coastal Swahili music with Arab, Indian, and Bantu roots; often performed at weddings and at cultural clubs in Zanzibar and Dar.
- **Ngoma** — traditional drumming; still a core part of weddings, religious ceremonies, and cultural festivals.
- **Hip-hop Tanzania**: Bantu Clan (traditional), Professor Jay, Mr. Nice, Block, Shetta, Fid Q, Mwana FA.
- **Major labels / collectives**: WCB Wasafi (Diamond Platnumz's label), Konde Musik (Harmonize, was WCB), African Princess, Mainstreet Records.
- **Live venues (Dar)**: Alliance Française Dar, Q-BAR, Bwawani, New Africa Hotel, Riviera, Mlimani City.
- **Live venues (Zanzibar)**: Forodhani Gardens (street food + live music in evening), Dhow Bar, Mercury House (Forodhani), Zanzibar Beach Resort amphitheatre, Old Fort stage.
- **Festivals**: Sauti za Busara (Zanzibar, Feb), Sange (Dar), Bongo Flavor Festival, Bagamoyo Arts Festival (sometimes).

## Major Artists (Quick Reference)

- **Diamond Platnumz** — East Africa's biggest crossover star. Hits: "Jeje," "Yope Remix," "Waah," "Kanyaga." WCB Wasafi label. Performances across Africa, Europe, US.
- **Ali Kiba** — one of the "Big Two" (with Diamond), sultry voice, "Mwana," "Dushelele."
- **Harmonize** — solo star after WCB. "Kwa Ngwaru," "Never Knew."
- **Zuchu** — Diamond's protégé. "Sukari," "Cheche."
- **Rayvanny** — WCB alumnus. "Tetema," "Number One."
- **Nandy** — feminine voice, "Pero," "Kaze Kaze."
- **Mbosso** — "Hodari," "Tamba."
- **Mabere** — singeli pioneer, "Kaseshele."
- **Mwana FA** — singeli godfather, "Kawawa."
- **Lava Lava** — WCB alumni, "Dushelele."
- **Ibraah, Anaty, Mijani Mijani** — newer wave.
- **Rose Muhando, Christina Shusho, Msanii** — leading gospel artists.

## Stats & Stats

- Spotify: 1+ million monthly listeners for top Tanzanian artists in 2024.
- Bongo flava has been the dominant streamed Swahili music genre, 60%+ of regional Swahili charts.
- Sauti za Busara: ~12,000–15,000 attendees, 4 days, 200+ acts from across Africa.

## Cultural Etiquette at Music Events

- Dress smart-casual; clubs enforce a "no sports wear" rule at night.
- Bouncers may ask for ID at popular clubs; the legal drinking age is 18.
- Photography of performers: ask first; many artists do not allow flash or unauthorized filming.
