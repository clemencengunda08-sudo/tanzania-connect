# Agriculture

Agriculture is the backbone of Tanzania's economy, contributing roughly 25–27% of GDP, employing ~65% of the workforce, and supplying the bulk of rural incomes and export earnings. The country is the world's second-largest producer of cashew nuts, historically the largest sisal producer, and a significant exporter of coffee, tea, tobacco, cotton, and sugar. Diverse agroecological zones support both smallholder subsistence farming and large-scale commercial estates.

## Key Facts

- **Six main cash crops**: cashew, coffee, cotton, sisal, tea, tobacco. Other major crops: sugar, pyrethrum, sunflower, sesame, palm oil, spices (cloves — Zanzibar is a major producer).
- **Cashew (Mkorosho)**: ~80% grown in Mtwara, Lindi, Ruvuma (southern zones). ~25% of smallholder income in those regions. Tanzania is the world's #2 producer after Ivory Coast.
- **Coffee**: ~70% Arabica, 30% Robusta. Arabica grown on slopes of Mt. Kilimanjaro, Mt. Meru, and in Mbeya/Iringa highlands (Bourbon, Kent, KP varieties). Robusta grown in Kagera region. Estimated 250,000+ smallholder families.
- **Tea**: Lush green belt — Mufindi (Luponde, Kibwambo) and Njombe (Luewa, Kilima) in the southern highlands, plus Tukuyu (Rungwe) and Muheza (Lushoto) in the west/north-east. Operated by estate companies and smallholders.
- **Sisal**: Tanzania was once the world's largest sisal producer (1960s–70s). Now ~3rd after Brazil and Kenya. Mainly Tanga and Morogoro regions. Six major estates: Katani Ltd, Tancord, Mlingano, others.
- **Tobacco**: grown in Tabora (ur flue-cured), Iringa, Mbeya. Tanzania Tobacco Processors Ltd (TTPL) is the major processor.
- **Sugar**: Kilombero (Kilombero Sugar Company), Mtibwa, Madhvani, TPC (Tanganyika Planting Company, Kagera). Annual national demand exceeds production by ~150,000 tonnes.
- **Cotton**: Mwanza, Shinyanga, Tabora, Singida, Kigoma — west-central zone. Contract farming via ginners.
- **Tanzania Agricultural Research Institute (TARI)** — has 16 research centres, leads seed and variety work.
- **Crop boards**: Cashewnut Board of Tanzania (CBT), Tanzania Coffee Board (TCB), Tanzania Tea Board (TTB), Tobacco Board, Sisal Board, Sugar Board — regulate their respective crops.

## Statistics

- Agriculture = ~26% of GDP, ~30% of exports, ~65% of employment.
- Tanzania produces **>15 million tonnes** of food crops per year; maize is the staple, ~6–7 million t/year.
- Coffee exports: ~80,000–100,000 t/year (arabica + robusta). Top destination: Japan, Italy, USA, Germany.
- Cashew exports: ~250,000 t/year (raw nuts, but govt policy is moving toward 100% local processing).
- Tea exports: ~30,000 t/year.
- Arable land: ~44 million ha; only ~33% currently cultivated.
- Smallholder farmers = ~80% of cultivated land; commercial estates = ~20%.
