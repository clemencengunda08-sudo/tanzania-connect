# Transport

Tanzania's transport network is dominated by road and rail links between the Indian Ocean port of Dar es Salaam and the landlocked interior (DRC, Rwanda, Burundi, Uganda, Zambia, Malawi). The new Standard Gauge Railway (SGR) is reshaping freight, Air Tanzania is rebuilding the national carrier's network, and ride-hailing apps have transformed urban mobility in Dar and Arusha. The country's position on the Indian Ocean makes it the natural gateway for East Africa's trade.

## Key Facts

### Air

- **Air Tanzania Company Limited (ATCL)** — national carrier, hub at JNIA, Dar. Fleet: Boeing 787-8 Dreamliner, 737 MAX 9, 737-500/200, Q400, DHC-8-300, Cessna Caravans. Routes: Dar ↔ Arusha, Bukoba, Dodoma, Mwanza, Kilimanjaro, Kigoma, Mbeya, Mtwara, Songea, Zanzibar; international: Entebbe, Lusaka, Harare, Bujumbura, Comoros, Mumbai, Guangzhou (historical), Johannesburg, Nairobi, Addis Ababa.
- **Major airlines** operating Tanzania: Ethiopian Airlines, Kenya Airways, KLM, Qatar Airways, Emirates, Turkish Airlines, RwandAir, Uganda Airlines, Precision Air, Auric Air (charter, safari).
- **International airports**: JNIA (Dar), KIA (Kilimanjaro, JRO, between Arusha and Moshi), AAKIA (Zanzibar, ZNZ), Songwe (MBI, southern highlands).
- **Domestic operators**: Precision Air, Auric Air, Coastal Aviation (bush flights to safari airstrips), Tropical Air, ZanAir.

### Road / bus

- **Long-distance express bus operators** (premium, AC, online booking):
  - **Kilimanjaro Express** (Dar↔Arusha/Moshi) — kilimanjaroexpress.com
  - **Dar Express** (Dar↔Mwanza/Mbeya/Dodoma)
  - **ABACUS Coach** (Dar↔Arusha, Mwanza, Kigoma)
  - **Royal Coach** (Dar↔Dodoma, Mbeya)
  - **Tahmeed Coach** (Dar↔Mwanza, Kigoma)
  - **Trinity Express** (international, Kigali, Kigoma, Kampala)
- **Dala-dala** (Swahili for local minibus) — ubiquitous in cities, TZS 400–1,000 within city; loud, slow, packed but the cheapest way to get around.
- **Bajaj / tuk-tuk** — three-wheelers, common in Arusha, Moshi, Tanga, Dodoma; typically TZS 1,000–3,000.
- **Bodaboda** (motorcycle taxi) — quick, risky. Always wear a helmet; agree fare before.

### Ride-hailing

- **Uber** — operates in Dar es Salaam, Arusha, Dodoma, Mwanza.
- **Bolt** — operates in Dar, Arusha, Dodoma.
- **Little (Wasoko) / Picka** — local Tanzanian apps.
- **Yassir** — North-African operator now in Dar.
- **Onyx Connect, Goko** — local options.

### Rail

- **TRC (Tanzania Railways Corporation)** — central corridor (Dar↔Kigoma), northern (Tanga↔Arusha) — meter gauge, scenic but slow.
- **Tanzania SGR** — standard gauge, electric, 200 km/h; **Dar ↔ Dodoma** opened 2024 with extensions ongoing. Operators running on TAZARA Central Line? TAZARA = Tanzania-Zambia Railway Authority (separate, 1,860 km, Dar↔New Kapiri-Mposhi, Zambia).

### Sea / ferry

- **Ferries across the harbour (Dar)** — Kigamboni–Kariakoo, Kigamboni–Soko la Mwendokasi. Modern MV Magogoni added 2018.
- **MV Liemba** — Lake Tanganyika passenger + cargo ferry, Kigoma ↔ Mpulungu (Zambia), legendary 1914 German vessel.
- **MV Victoria** (Mwanza ↔ Bukoba ↔ Kemondo Bay) — Lake Victoria ferry.
- **High-speed ferries (Zanzibar)** — Azam Marine, Fast Ferries, Sechafer, Flying Horse: Dar↔Zanzibar in 2–4 hours.

## Statistics

- Dar es Salaam port: ~95% of Tanzania's seaborne trade; ~28 million tonnes/year.
- Tanzania's road network: ~33,000 km trunk + 80,000 km district + 7,000 km regional.
- ATCL domestic market share: ~25–30% of scheduled seats; #1 on key long-distance routes.
- Uber/Bolt: each ~5,000–8,000 active drivers in Dar; >70% mobile-payment users.
