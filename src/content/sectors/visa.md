# Visa & Immigration

Tanzania issues a single, fully-online eVisa covering tourism, business, transit, and most other short-stay categories. The system is run by the Immigration Department of the Ministry of Home Affairs and the application portal at visa.immigration.go.tz is the only official channel — third-party sites are resellers that add fees.

## Key Facts

- **Portal**: visa.immigration.go.tz (the only official Tanzania eVisa portal).
- **Fee categories (USD, non-refundable, payable online)**:
  - **Ordinary (single-entry tourist) Visa** — USD 50
  - **Multiple-entry Visa** — USD 100 (mandatory for U.S. citizens for tourism; valid 12 months)
  - **Business Visa** — USD 250 (up to 90 days, requires host company letter)
  - **Transit Visa** — USD 30
  - **East Africa Tourist Visa** (Kenya + Uganda + Tanzania) — USD 100
- **Processing time**: 2–10 working days; submit at least 2 weeks before travel.
- **Validity**: Ordinary visa = 90 days, single entry. Multiple-entry = 12 months / 90-day-per-stay.
- **Required documents**: passport bio page (≥6 months validity, ≥2 blank pages), return ticket, accommodation booking, yellow-fever certificate (if arriving from endemic country), recent passport-size photo.
- **Visa on arrival**: not currently available — must apply online in advance; the printed approval letter is presented at the immigration desk on arrival.
- **Gratis / special**: SADC nationals (12+ countries) are issued a 30-day visitor's permit on arrival; EAC citizens (Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan, DRC) get 6-month visitor's pass on arrival with national ID.

## Statistics

- **Approx. 1.7+ million** tourist arrivals per year (Tanzania, 2023–24 estimates, pre-pandemic recovered).
- Top source markets: Kenya, USA, UK, Italy, Germany, France, India, China, Zambia, Uganda.
- E-visa processing success rate is high (~95%+) when documents are complete; rejections are typically due to poor-quality scans or missing yellow-fever certificate.
- Multiple-entry visa uptake has been growing as the U.S. requirement raises awareness.
