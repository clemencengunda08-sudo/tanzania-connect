# Corporate & Investment

Tanzania is positioning itself as a leading investment destination in East Africa through a single-window agency (TISEZA), generous EPZ/SEZ fiscal incentives, and one-stop registration portals. Major reforms since 2023 have consolidated the former Tanzania Investment Centre (TIC) and the Export Processing Zones Authority (EPZA) into the Tanzania Investment and Special Economic Zones Authority (TISEZA), streamlining licensing and aftercare services for foreign and domestic investors.

## Key Facts

- **TISEZA (Tanzania Investment and Special Economic Zones Authority)** — the umbrella one-stop shop, formed 2023 by merging the former **TIC** (Tanzania Investment Centre) and **EPZA**. Issues Certificate of Incentives, facilitates licensing, and runs special economic zones.
- **BRELA (Business Registrations and Licensing Agency)** — handles company registration, trademarks, business names. Integrated with the Online Registration System (ORS) at ors.brela.go.tz.
- **TPDC, TANROADS, TPA, TANESCO, TTCL** — each sector SOE that interfaces with investors.
- **Investment incentives (general)**: 
  - Reduced corporate tax (25% standard; **20% for new manufacturing**).
  - 100% capital allowance on plant/machinery (2-year write-down).
  - Import duty + VAT deferment on capital goods.
- **EPZ / SEZ incentives** (Export Processing Zone / Special Economic Zone):
  - 10-year corporate tax holiday.
  - Exemption from customs duty, VAT, and withholding tax on imported raw materials, capital goods, and replacement parts.
  - 100% foreign ownership allowed.
  - Free repatriation of profits, dividends, capital.
  - 50% initial allowance on qualifying building investment.
  - Exemption from withholding tax on dividends for first 10 years.
- **Key SEZs**: Bagamoyo SEZ (in development), Kisongo SEZ (Arusha), Manyoni SEZ, Kigamboni SEZ, Mtwara SEZ.
- **Land**: held in leasehold from the state (up to 99 years); TIC/TISEZA helps foreign investors navigate the land alienation process.
- **Tanzania Diaspora Window** — special facilitation for Tanzanians abroad investing back home.
- **Tanzania One Stop Facilitation Centre (OSFC)** — single-window for visas, work permits, residence permits, tax IDs (TIN), and business licensing in Dar.

## Statistics

- FDI inflows: ~USD 1.1–1.7 billion/year (2022–24 estimates, recovery from COVID).
- Top source countries: China, UK, USA, India, South Africa, UAE.
- Mining contributes ~7% of GDP and ~50% of exports; gold, tanzanite, gemstones, nickel, rare earths.
- Tanzania is the world's only source of **tanzanite** (Merelani Hills, Manyara Region).
- Major recent deals: Dangote Cement ($1B expansion), BHP/BAR-Gemfields, Sukari Minerals (gold), Barrick Gold (Buzwagi + North Mara), Graphite Africa, Chinese-financed Bagamoyo port deal (suspended).
