# **App Name**: Tanzania Connect

## Core Features:

- AI-Powered Local Assistant: An intelligent AI tool to provide instant answers and advice on Tanzanian culture, regulations, safety, and general foreigner queries.
- Essential Info Guides: Curated articles and guides covering crucial information like visa requirements, healthcare access, local etiquette, safety tips, and currency exchange.
- Communication Directory: A centralized directory for essential contacts, including emergency services, foreign embassies, and recommended local service providers.
- Transport & Navigation Overview: Guidance on local transport options (e.g., Dala-dalas, taxis, ride-hailing), common routes, estimated fares, and navigation safety tips.
- Basic Swahili Phrasebook: An interactive phrasebook offering essential Swahili phrases and common vocabulary for daily interactions, categorized for easy access.
- Agricultural Insights (Overview): General information and resources on Tanzania's agricultural sector, including basic farming practices, local produce, and introductory market insights.
- Healthcare & Emergency Services: Comprehensive information on accessing medical facilities, emergency contacts, health insurance guidance, and common health concerns in Tanzania.
- Accommodation & Housing Guide: Resources for finding short-term and long-term accommodation, including hotel listings, rental options, and tips for securing safe housing.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A deep, earthy green (#388E3C) to symbolize Tanzania's lush landscapes and natural beauty, providing a calming and welcoming feel.
- Background color: A subtle, light green-tinted off-white (#F0F4EF) to ensure readability and an airy, fresh user experience.
- Accent color: A vibrant golden yellow (#FFC107) to highlight important information, calls to action, and convey warmth and energy, complementing the primary green.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for a modern, tech-forward, and clean aesthetic, providing good contrast for titles and headings.
- Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for clear readability and a neutral, objective presentation of detailed information, suitable for extensive guides.
- Utilize a consistent set of clean, minimalist line icons for navigation, categories, and key actions to enhance intuitive understanding and streamline the user interface.
- A card-based layout featuring clear headings and ample spacing will organize information effectively, making content digestible and easy to navigate on mobile devices.
- Implement subtle, functional animations for transitions between sections and interactive elements to provide positive feedback and enhance the fluidity of the user experience.
